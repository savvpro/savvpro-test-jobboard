### 1. Browsing & Searching Jobs

- Go to the **Jobs** page.
- Use filters to narrow down results:
  - Department
  - Location (Remote / Hybrid / Onsite)
  - Salary Range
  - Experience Level
- Sort by: Newest, Salary (High to Low), Deadline

### 2. Viewing Job Details

- Click on any job card from the listing.
- You will see:
  - Full job description
  - Salary range
  - Required skills
  - Location & Deadline
  - Number of applicants (if visible to HR)

### 3. Applying for a Job

1. Open the desired job.
2. Click **"Apply Now"** button.
3. Fill in the application form:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Years of Experience
   - CV Summary (max 1000 characters)
   - LinkedIn Profile (optional)
4. Click **Submit Application**.

> **Note**: You cannot apply twice to the same job using the same email.

### 4. Managing Applications (HR / Admin)

- Navigate to **"All Applications"** or go to a specific job and click **"View Applications"**.
- You can filter applications by:
  - Status (`Pending`, `Shortlisted`, `Offered`, `Rejected`)
  - Date
  - Experience

**Screenshot Placeholder:**
![Applications Dashboard](./screenshots/applications-dashboard.png)

---

### 5. Updating Application Status

1. Open any application.
2. Click **"Change Status"**.
3. Select new status:
   - Shortlisted
   - Offered
   - Rejected
4. **Write a note** (mandatory) explaining the reason.
5. Click **Update**.

**Screenshot Placeholder:**
![Update Status](./screenshots/update-status.png)

---

### 6. Viewing Status History

- Every status change is recorded.
- Open any application → Scroll to **"Status History"** section.
- You can see complete timeline:
  - Previous Status
  - New Status
  - Note / Reason
  - Date & Time

## Additional Tips

- **Deadline & Closed Jobs**: You cannot apply to closed or expired jobs.
- **Max Applicants**: Some jobs have a limit. Once reached, new applications are rejected.
- **Soft Delete**: Deleted records are hidden from normal users.
- Always provide meaningful notes when changing application status.
